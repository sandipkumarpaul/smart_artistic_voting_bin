Workshop Presenter's Guide

STEM Concepts & Talking Points

1Upcycling vs Downcycling

Standard recycling degrades plastic quality. Upcycling transforms low-value waste into a high-value community smart asset — worth more than the sum of its parts.

2Nudge Theory & Gamified Recycling

Fun prompts ("Messi vs Ronaldo") convert passive littering into an interactive game. Students clean the environment while expressing preferences — behavioral economics in action!

3EEE Hardware Stack

  • HX711 Load Cells: Strain-gauge sensors measuring ±2g accuracy
  • IR Proximity Sensors: Contactless bottle detection
  • SG90 Servos: Motorized anti-tamper gate flaps
  • ESP32 MCU: Wi-Fi/BT telemetry to cloud

4Circular Economy & Social Impact

Plastic mass → direct BDT income for campus janitorial staff, creating equitable local micro-economies around every bin.
